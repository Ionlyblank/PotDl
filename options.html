Good to know

  • Open a video: right-click a link and choose Open in PotPlayer.
  • Wrong subtitle? Click the PotDl icon and pick another one. The video keeps playing.
  • Wrong video? Click the pencil in that list to edit the title, season or episode.
  • Subtitle not selected? In PotPlayer, press F5, open Subtitles, then Language/Sync/Other, and set Subtitle priority to Prefer external subtitles.
  • Removing PotDl: uninstall PotDl helper from Installed apps in Windows Settings.
